StallFree is a frontend-only demonstration. Accounts, orders and payments are simulated and stored in this browser. No real payment or delivery is processed.

Delivery

Free delivery over ₱1,000

Orders below ₱1,000 carry a flat ₱75 nationwide fee. Your summary always shows the delivery fee before you confirm the order.

Processing times

Each listing shows its processing time — made-to-order pieces take longer than ready-to-ship ones. Estimated arrival appears on your order after checkout.

Tracking your order

Your simulated order status and estimated delivery date live in Your order. An administrator can advance the demo status; no courier is contacted.

Payment methods

Cash on delivery (demo)

Choose this option to simulate paying a rider on arrival. No rider, parcel, charge, or delivery is real in this demo.

GCash (demo)

A simulated payment choice only. No gateway is contacted, no reference number is sent, and no money moves.

Returns & replacements

Damaged on arrival?

Message the shop from its storefront page within 3 days of delivery with a photo — verified stalls are required to replace or refund damaged items.

Change of mind

Because most pieces are made to order, returns for change of mind are accepted only when the listing's policy says so. Check the policy row on each shop page.

Buyer protection (demo)

In a real marketplace this would trigger a refund process. Here the order simply changes to Cancelled and stock is released in local demo data.

Privacy

StallFree is a front-end demo that runs entirely in your browser. In plain terms:

  • Accounts, carts, orders, and profiles are stored in this browser's localStorage — never on a server.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed; the plain text is never stored.
  • Payment details are not collected: checkout records only your chosen payment method.
  • Deleting your browser data (or using “Delete all local data” on the profile page) removes everything.
  • Nothing is transmitted to any third party — product photos are the only external requests.

Frequently asked questions

Is my payment information stored anywhere?

No. This is a fully client-side demo — there is no server, no payment gateway, and nothing is transmitted. Cart, orders, and profile details stay in your browser's localStorage.

Can I use the site without an account?

Yes. Everything works straight away: search, save items, add to cart, and check out. Your profile is optional and only used to pre-fill checkout details.

Do discount codes really work?

Yes — try WELCOME10 (10% off), STALL50 (₱50 off orders over ₱500), or FREESHIP (waives delivery). Apply them in the cart summary before placing your order.

Where do the product photos come from?

Listing photos are served from Unsplash and every URL used in this project has been verified. If a photo cannot load, the card falls back to a coloured tile with the item's initial, so nothing ever breaks.

How do I clear my data?

Open your profile and use “Delete all local data” at the bottom of the page.

Who can access the administrator dashboard?

Exactly one administrator can be created, through the one-time setup on admin.html in a fresh browser profile. All public sign-ups are customers. This is a UI/data-integrity rule only: anyone with developer tools can edit browser storage, so it is not real security.

How do I see or change my review?

Reviews appear on the product page and are saved only in this browser for the account (or guest session) that wrote them. Your own review is marked Your review and has an Edit review link that updates it in place instead of adding a duplicate. Switching accounts hides other accounts' reviews and edits. There is no moderation service or shared review database.

How do orders and status updates work in this demo?

Checking out creates a saved demo order for the signed-in account (or the guest session) and shows it under Your order. The administrator can move the order through the demo statuses — placed, confirmed, preparing, shipped and delivered — and any change appears on the customer's history after a reload. No courier is contacted and no real shipping progress is invented.

How do I reset the demo or back it up?

The administrator dashboard can export a JSON backup of demo orders and catalogue edits; account passwords are never exported. To reset this browser completely, use “Delete all local data” on your profile and then clear this site's browser storage. Deleting browser data also deletes local demo accounts and orders and cannot be undone.
